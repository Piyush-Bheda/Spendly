Privacy Policy

1. Data We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when you create an account, such as your email address and password. Additionally, we store the financial transactions and categories you enter into the app to provide the tracking service.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to provide the core functionality of Spendly. This includes calculating your spending patterns, generating reports, and managing your account authentication.

3. Data Storage

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. Your information is stored in a secure database and is never sold or shared with third-party marketers.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not currently integrate with third-party financial institutions. All data is manually entered by the user, ensuring you have full control over what information is stored.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us via the support email provided in your account settings.
